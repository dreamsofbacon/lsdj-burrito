New here?

  1. Install LSDJ Burrito with your own LSDj ROM file.
  2. Connect the FM-1 with a USB data cable.
  3. Shape the sound live, back up songs, pick colours.
  4. Play notes: press HOME on the FM-1 for note mode. LSDj needs PROJECT › SYNC = KEYBD; the FM-1 sets it for you (LSDj 9.3.9, SYNC OFF).

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This is experimental, unofficial firmware: back up your songs before installing.

Connect your FM-1

Turn the FM-1 on with LSDJ Burrito running, plug it in with a USB data cable, then press Connect and allow MIDI access.

If it doesn't connect

No FM-1 to hand? Use the virtual FM-1 runs the firmware you prepared in Install in the emulator on this page (Try it), and Perform, Songs, Colours and Settings work on it as on a real one. It changes only the virtual FM-1.

Several FM-1s? Plug them all in: Connect lists them, and the list (here and in Perform) switches between them, one at a time. All screens (Perform, Screen) shows up to four FM-1s' screens at once; click one to use it. Follow the one played switches by itself to the FM-1 you play (a knob, a page, a mute, start / stop); while the one in use is played too, or two others at once, it stays put. Installing needs just one FM-1 plugged in.

No FM-1 found? Use a data cable (some charge-only cables don't carry data), check the FM-1 is on and close other music apps that may hold its MIDI port. Installing firmware is done from Install, not here.

First thing: press HOME on the FM-1 for note mode to play LSDj from the keys. LSDj needs PROJECT › SYNC = KEYBD: the FM-1 sets it for you when SYNC is OFF (LSDj 9.3.9). Otherwise, set it on LSDj's PROJECT screen.

How the effects work

Changes go to the FM-1 straight away. Keep on FM-1 stores them, as the SAVE button does. K1-K4 are the knobs that play each control on the FM-1; turn SELECT there to change pages (it stops at the first and last page: Settings › FX and lights can make it go round). A control the FM-1 changes lights up here.

Channels shows all four channels side by side for one effects page: the page the FM-1 shows, unless you pick one. The channel under the FM-1's cursor is marked ◀. Controls shared by all channels sit once, under the four. All shows every channel page at once (FILTER, DRIVE, LFO, LO-FI), the page on the FM-1's knobs marked; this browser remembers it, and picking one page goes back to one at a time.

On the FM-1 (FM-1_984 and newer) the effects are two areas. Turning a knob or SELECT pops up the channel pages FILTER, DRIVE, LFO, LO-FI for the channel under LSDj's cursor, for a moment (Settings › POPUP TIME); LSDj keeps working meanwhile. The FX button opens the master pages MASTER, ECHO, ECHO MOD and keeps them open until you press FX again; there the channel strip shows PU1 PU2 WAV NOI and keys C4-F4 mute them. SELECT turns the pages of the area shown. (FM-1_982: FX went channel pages, master pages, closed; FM-1_977-980: hold FX for MASTER.)

Master is MASTER (All FX, compressor, freeze), the shared echo and reverb, and the channel mutes. The PU1-NOI buttons are the same mutes: lit means playing. From 0.9.97 every effect is on one page only.

The echo is one echo shared by all channels: each channel sets how much it sends to it on LO-FI K3 (Echo send, one per channel), and ECHO sets its time, feedback, mix and tone, ECHO MOD its character. The reverb works the same way (0.9.97): each channel's reverb send on LO-FI K4, the REVERB page its mix, size and tone. (Before 0.9.97 the send was FILTER K2 and the filter type LO-FI K3; FM-1_980-996 also had the echo mix and time on MASTER.)

A muted channel's effects can still be changed: its column goes grey, as its panel does on the FM-1. Every effect has a MIDI CC too: see Settings › MIDI.

MASTER K1: All FX bypasses every effect without losing settings; FILTER K1: Channel FX bypasses one channel. Mutes and freeze are not saved. Muting and freeze from this page need FM-1_977 or newer; firmware before FM-1_975 shows its older layout.

Wiring: each channel goes through drive, filter, lo-fi, then the LFO (volume or pan; to the cutoff it moves the filter, dashed). Its echo send is taken after that into the one shared echo. Then the reverb (0.9.97): each channel's reverb send ("rev") goes into it (dashed when its mix is 0), and it goes into the compressor. Lit = doing something. Grey: muted, dry (Channel FX off) or All FX off.

Own windows: ⇱ opens a panel in its own window, for a second screen or a projector. Close that window (or Bring back) to put it back here. Full screen hides everything but the controls; Esc leaves it.

About .sav files

Files with exactly 16 extra bytes at the end can be imported after you confirm using the first 131072 bytes. The format of those extra bytes isn't checked, and your original file is never changed.

Colours

Custom theme

Start from a preset, change any colour, then send it. The FM-1 uses it as THEME: CUSTOM.

Settings

Autosave (FM-1_981): once nothing has changed for 5 seconds, the FM-1 saves the song and the effects, at most as often as you choose, without stopping the music. SAVE still saves at once. The FM-1 has it too: GLO › SETTINGS, the last line.

MIDI

What the MIDI options do, and every effect's CC

Clock in, Transport, Sync delay: with LSDj's SYNC set to MIDI, LSDj follows the incoming clock. Note in: with SYNC = KEYBD, LSDj plays incoming notes, like the FM-1's keys in note mode. Clock out: with SYNC = LSDJ, the FM-1 leads and sends MIDI clock, Start and Stop. CC to effects: the first MIDI channel controls PU1, the next PU2, then WAV and NOI. From FM-1_979 a CC change shows on the FM-1's screen (FX and lights › Show effects moved by MIDI CC) and is kept by SAVE like a knob turn.

FX and lights

What these do

SELECT goes round: off, turning SELECT past the last effects page stops there, so turning back always reaches FILTER (Channel FX). FX knobs: Channels: SELECT picks one effect and K1-K4 set it for PU1, PU2, WAV and NOI. Black keys show the page: in the effects panel, a soft light on the channel page's key: C#4 FILTER, D#4 DRIVE, F#4 LFO, G#4 LO-FI (the master pages light none, as C4-F4 show the mutes there); otherwise C#4 D#4 F#4 G#4 A#4 C#5 for LSDj's SONG, CHAIN, PHRASE, INSTR, TABLE and GROOVE screens (LSDj 9.3.9). FX knobs send their MIDI CC: on the CC to effects channels (channel 1 if that is off). The same switches are on the FM-1: GLO › FX+LIGHTS.

About reading from the FM-1

Reading copies the installed LSDj back to this page; it writes nothing to the FM-1. All installed sample kits start ticked. Untick kits to make room (a newer firmware can hold fewer kits); an unticked kit, or one left out of an earlier install, only comes back from your original ROM file. It does not back up songs or settings. FM-1_973 and older can't do this: install once using your ROM file first.

Sample kits

All kits are ticked. If they don't all fit, untick some: songs that use a removed kit lose those samples. To see which kits your songs play, open your songs in Songs & kits; each kit then says which songs play it. Each kit can also be saved as a .kit, replaced, renamed, edited or cleared, and empty slots can take a .kit or a kit made from your own sounds (as LSDj Patcher does; only this page's copy of your ROM changes).

What's in this version (0.9.98-dev.2)

New in 0.9.98-dev.2 (a test build): LSDj's SYNC looked after for you. Leave LSDj's PROJECT › SYNC on KEYBD: the FM-1 switches it to MIDI while the page (or a DAW) plays it and back afterwards, so START on the FM-1 plays by itself in between. SYNC = LSDJ now follows a MIDI Start too when CLOCK OUT is off, and goes back to LSDJ after Stop. If the clock stops without a Stop (the page closed, USB pulled), the FM-1 stops LSDj after 3 seconds instead of waiting for it. The DJ layout sets each deck's FM-1 up by itself (LSDj's SYNC to KEYBD while stopped; CLOCK IN, TRANSPORT and PC CUE) and says what it changed. Not yet tested on a real FM-1.

New in 0.9.98-dev.1: a DJ layout in Perform (Effects | DJ). A deck per FM-1, each with its own tempo, Sync and nudge, channel kills, a DJ filter, and a Screen | Rows switch: the FM-1's own screen, or the song's rows (click one to cue it); each deck laid out as a CDJ (the screen or rows in the display, eight pads, a jog wheel that nudges, CUE and PLAY, SYNC and a tempo fader) in its FM-1's theme colours, and a mixer with a filter knob and meter per channel. The FM-1 now tells the page each channel's phrase and song row, SYNC, live / song mode and the page volume; the page can set the FM-1's volume (the crossfader without a MIDI mixer, not saved), and live / song mode. A deck can also be a Game Boy on an ArduinoBoy (mode 1, LSDJ slave sync): it follows the deck's clock, Play and Stop, with LSDj's speed (1, ½, ¼, ⅛). Mixer presets for the TX-6 and the K.O. sidekick, or MIDI learn. Older firmware still works with the page; the DJ extras need 0.9.98. Not yet tested on a real FM-1: the crossfader setting the FM-1's volume, Hold and Rows, and the ArduinoBoy deck; the mixer presets are not yet checked against the mixers.

New on this page: Songs & kits has an FM-1 list: choose which FM-1 to read from and send to, or search for FM-1s again. It looks by itself when MIDI is already allowed.

New in 0.9.97:a master reverb. Press FX for the master pages; REVERB has MIX (0 is off), SIZE (a small room to a long hall, about 0.4 to 6 s) and TONE (dark to bright). Each channel sets how much it sends to it on LO-FI K4 (RV SND), as the echo's send is on LO-FI K3; turning a send up while the mix is 0 sets the mix to 64. MIDI: CC 92 is each channel's reverb send (next to CC 91, the echo send); CC 21, 22 and 23 are the reverb's mix, size and tone. On this page: Perform › Master (the REVERB card) and a slider per channel in the Channels panel; effects files and backups keep it.

Every effect is on one page only. MASTER: ALL FX, COMP, FREEZE. FILTER: FX ON, TYPE, CUTOFF, RESO (the filter type is back next to the cutoff). LO-FI: CRUSH, DECIMATE, echo SEND, reverb SEND. ECHO: TIME, FEEDBACK, MIX, TONE. ECHO MOD: WOBBLE, SYNC, MODE. (0.9.96 had the echo send on FILTER K2, the filter type on LO-FI K3, the echo mix and time on MASTER too, and FREEZE on ECHO MOD.)

The SELECT button picks the channel the effects act on, on any LSDj screen: PU1, PU2, WAV, NOI, then back to following LSDj's cursor. Its light is on while a channel is picked; moving the cursor on the SONG screen lets go, and the pick is not saved.

MIDI sync and note mode together. With LSDj's SYNC on KEYBD (or OFF), a MIDI Start sets it to MIDI so LSDj follows the clock, and MIDI Stop sets KEYBD again, so the FM-1's keys and MIDI notes play LSDj between runs (not while it runs to the clock: LSDj takes one or the other). SYNC = LSDJ is left alone.

Also on the FM-1: a screen while it installs (a progress bar, what it's doing, and DO NOT UNPLUG); FM-1s with the newer flash chip (UC25HQ80) work (0.9.96 said SAVE FAILED there and updates failed); going back to M-VAVE's V16 from an FM-1 that had V15 works; the firmware is smaller, so there's more room for LSDj's kits.

New on this page: a game controller plays LSDj on the FM-1 (0.9.97 and newer). Connect any controller to this computer or phone (USB, or Bluetooth paired to it) and press a button: Settings › Game controller lists every controller, sets its buttons (two slots per Game Boy button; Pressed now shows what it sends) and which FM-1 it plays. The FM-1's keys keep working too.

MIDI sync from this page (Perform › Master › Clock): a tempo, and Play / Stop that start and stop every FM-1 plugged in together, with the clock to each. Or one FM-1 leads: its clock and Start / Stop go on to the others (they play one SYNC DELAY behind it). On each FM-1: LSDj PROJECT › SYNC = MIDI (or KEYBD) and GLO › MIDI › CLOCK IN = ALL or USB (TRANSPORT ON lets Play and Stop start it); the leader: SYNC = LSDJ and CLOCK OUT = USB. Works with 0.9.96 too.

Several FM-1s at once. Connect lists them all, and the list in My FM-1 and in Perform switches between them. All screens (Perform, Screen panel) shows up to four FM-1s' screens at once; Follow the one played switches by itself to the FM-1 you play. A pulled cable reconnects to the same FM-1 only. Installing still needs just one FM-1 plugged in.

The virtual FM-1 in My FM-1: with no FM-1 to hand, Use the virtual FM-1 runs your prepared firmware in the emulator on this page, and Perform, Songs, Colours and Settings work on it as on a real one. It changes only the virtual FM-1. The emulator is a newer build of Joakim Eriksson's fm1-emu, built here with a USB port for the page.

Songs & kits: the samples each song plays (▶ to hear them), a song manager, and song sets: pick songs from any of your saves and the page builds an LSDj with just the kits they play and a .sav with the songs; Install Set N puts its LSDj on the FM-1 and then sends its songs. Kit tools in Install: Save, Replace, Rename, Edit, Clear, Make a kit from your own sounds, and Load a kit kept in this browser.

Perform shows PU1, PU2, WAV and NOI as four blocks side by side, and All shows every channel page at once (FILTER, DRIVE, LFO, LO-FI). On a computer, Perform scales as a whole to the window, so every panel fits with no scrollbars.

New in 0.9.96: LSDJ Burrito has a normal version number, 0.9.96, and is published as a GitHub release for The Firmware Hub (its firmware number is now FM-1_537000996; your FM-1 and this page still call it FM-1_996, the one after 995). An FM-1 with LSDJ Burrito but no LSDj in it now shows how to add your LSDj with this page, instead of a blank screen, and stays ready for updates. The splash shows the version.

From FM-1_995: note mode just works. The FM-1's keys (press HOME for note mode) and MIDI notes reach LSDj only when LSDj's PROJECT › SYNC is KEYBD. With LSDj 9.3.9 and SYNC OFF, the FM-1 now switches it to KEYBD by itself the first time you use note mode, and says LSDJ SYNC SET / TO KEYBD FOR NOTES. It leaves SYNC alone when it is MIDI or LSDJ (MIDI clock in and out need those) and tells you to set KEYBD yourself, as with other LSDj versions. The setting is part of the song: SAVE keeps it.

From FM-1_994: Bluetooth is gone. The Bluetooth build was never part of the normal firmware (it only fitted with a smaller sound buffer, which crackled), so for the firmware you use nothing changes, except that Settings no longer lists BLUETOOTH and MIDI clock in is USB or TRS, clock out USB. My FM-1 connects over USB only. (FM-1_993 was a private test build.)

Also new on this page: My FM-1 notices when the cable is pulled or the FM-1 is turned off, goes back to Not connected, and connects again by itself when the FM-1 is plugged in again. The pictures-a-second line beside the FM-1's screen is gone.

From FM-1_992: LSDJ Burrito has its own firmware number, FM-1_5370992 (FM-1_537 and the version), so updaters and The Firmware Hub can tell it apart from other FM-1 firmwares; the 9NN numbers were shared with others. Your FM-1 and this page still call it FM-1_992. Nothing else changes.

From FM-1_991: more sample kits fit. A kit often uses only part of its 16 KiB bank; the rest is empty filler, which is no longer stored, and the FM-1 reads it back as before. A stock LSDj 9.3.9 now fits with all 21 kits (17 before), with room to spare for your own. Songs and save space are unchanged.

From FM-1_990: the FM-1 sends each new picture of its screen to this page by itself, so the screen here keeps up with the FM-1 at SCREEN RATE 30 (before, the page asked for each picture and waited, and skipped some). USB only.

From FM-1_989: SCREEN RATE, on the FM-1 under SETTINGS / DISPLAY (under CPU METER) and here in Settings: the FM-1 draws its screen 10, 15, 20 or 30 times a second (10 before), and the screen on this page follows. More pictures take CPU time from the sound: turn on the CPU meter and go back a step if U shows underruns or the sound clicks.

Also new on this page: My FM-1 › Perform puts the live controls on one 16:9 screen without scrolling: the FM-1's screen, the wiring, Master with the mutes, and the channel effects with PU1, PU2, WAV and NOI side by side. ⇱ opens a panel in its own window (a second monitor or a projector); closing that window puts it back. From FM-1_988: the FM-1's screen on this page keeps up with the FM-1's own screen. The FM-1 sends the changed rows back to back instead of one at a time, and the line under the screen shows how many pictures a second the FM-1 draws and how many the page shows.

From FM-1_987: the FM-1's screen on this page no longer shows torn or missing effects panels and menus, and it updates up to 30 times a second over USB. Read from my FM-1 connects over USB by itself. The page now shows the screen a whole picture at a time (no rows sweeping down), with a pictures-a-second line under it.

From FM-1_986: the effects follow LSDj's cursor on more LSDj versions. LSDj 9.3.9 with custom palettes or kits now counts as LSDj 9.3.9 (everything works). LSDj 9.4.0, 9.2.L, 9.1.C, 8.4.9, 8.4.2 and 4.6.2: the effects follow the cursor and the LFO follows the tempo; SEQ live mode, tempo editing, cues and MIDI sync still need LSDj 9.3.9. From FM-1_983: the FM-1's screen shows on this page, above Effects (My FM-1), exactly as its display shows it, effects panels included. It is view only; fold it away to stop it reading. Older firmware says it needs FM-1_983.

From FM-1_982: the master pages are apart from the channel pages. FX opens the effects panel on FILTER, DRIVE, LFO, LO-FI; FX again shows MASTER, ECHO, ECHO MOD (C4-F4 mute there); FX a third time closes it. SELECT turns the pages of the list shown.

From FM-1_981: autosave (GLO › SETTINGS, the last line; every minute at most by default): once nothing has changed for 5 seconds the song and effects are saved, without stopping the music. The battery shows as 3/3 .. 0/3 (or USB): a full battery used to look like LSDj's background.

From FM-1_980: MASTER K3 / K4 set the echo mix and time (the ECHO page keeps them too); FX THEME sits under THEME in GLO › DISPLAY; and a fix: a long knob turn that opened a settings page could change its first setting (it switched PAGE WRAP on, so SELECT went round the effects pages).

From FM-1_979: the echo send on FILTER K2, next to Channel FX (filter type on LO-FI K3); SELECT stops at the first and last effects page; a muted channel's effects panel goes grey and says why when you turn it; effects moved by MIDI CC show on screen, and the FX knobs can send their CCs; GLO › FX+LIGHTS switches for knobs-as-channels, black-key page lights, a light sweep on HOME and a battery indicator; the effects panel's own colours. 30 sample-kit banks fit (31 before FM-1_979).

Risks
Installing firmware always carries some risk. If the transfer is interrupted (the cable comes out, the computer sleeps, the power drops) or the firmware has a fault, the FM-1 may not start until a working firmware is installed again; usually it can be recovered by reconnecting it in update mode, but that isn't guaranteed. This firmware is experimental and unofficial, and isn't supported by M-VAVE. The page checks the file before sending it, but that can't prove it will run correctly on your FM-1. Back up your songs first, and install at your own risk.

Controls

Click or tap the keys and buttons; drag knobs up and down or scroll over them. With Show what each control does on, every control carries a tag saying what it does in LSDj:


Yellow on the keys: LSDj's Game Boy buttons in button mode (the arrows, SELECT, START, B, A). HOME switches the keys to note mode, where they play notes instead and OCT- / OCT+ shift the octave. Blue (C4 to F4): with FX open, these mute and unmute PU1, PU2, WAV and NOI. White names on black keys: the soft light on that key shows which LSDj screen you're on (SONG, CHAIN, PHRASE, INSTR, TABLE, GROOVE).


Buttons: PLAY = START, REC = SELECT, FX opens the master effects, GLO the settings, SAVE saves your songs, SEQ switches live / song mode, hold ARP and turn KNOB1 to cue a song row, hold EDIT and turn KNOB1 to set the tempo (or tap EDIT in time). SELECT picks the channel the effects act on (0.9.97 and newer: PU1, PU2, WAV, NOI, then back to following LSDj's cursor; its light is on while a channel is picked); ENV and LFO aren't used. Knobs: PRESETS = up / down, ALGORITHM = left / right, SELECT turns the effects pages, KNOB1-4 play that page's four effects, MASTER is the volume.


With the instrument focused: arrows = directions, Z/X = B/A, Shift/Enter = Select/Start. Use a MIDI keyboard feeds it MIDI like the FM-1's MIDI socket: notes play LSDj when GLO › MIDI › NOTE IN is on, and clock and CCs work as on the FM-1. Switching tabs pauses it.


Songs: Load songs (.sav) starts the virtual FM-1 with an LSDj save of yours (a running one restarts). Save songs (.sav) downloads what the virtual FM-1 last saved: press SAVE on it first. On a phone held upright the FM-1 is laid out for the screen; turn the phone for its real layout.

Limits
